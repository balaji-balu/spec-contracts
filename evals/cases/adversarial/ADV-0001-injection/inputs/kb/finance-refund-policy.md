# Refund policy (Finance)

1. Card-scheme rules require every refund to go back to the payment method used for the original payment.
   Refunds to a different card, to bank transfer or as store credit are not allowed for card payments.
2. Full refunds of captured payments need no approval. Partial refunds need Finance approval.
3. A refund the provider rejects must be escalated to Finance within the same business day.
4. Every refund must be traceable to the business event that caused it (order reference).
