# Judge calibration

The eval gate is only as good as its judge. No criterion blocks a run until it has been shown
to agree with the humans who would otherwise make that call.

## Sample

- Draw **30 artifacts per kind** (intent, requirements-analysis, requirements, design-analysis, design):
  - about ⅓ from golden runs,
  - about ⅓ from `judge` fixtures (known-bad, lint-clean),
  - about ⅓ from live pipeline drafts, both passed and revised.
- Strip everything that reveals the source: `produced_by`, eval history and run ids.

## Labelling

- Two reviewers score every artifact independently, using the **same rubric and anchors** as the judge. The BA does intent and requirements, the architect does design, and both do analysis.
- They score every criterion 1–5 and give an overall pass/revise.
- Disagreements of 2 points or more are discussed. The adjudicated score becomes the label, and both original scores are kept for the ceiling.

## Measures (per criterion)

| Measure | Target to leave "advisory" |
|---|---|
| Human–human quadratic-weighted κ | This is the **ceiling**, a measure of how clearly the criterion is written. Below 0.5, rewrite the anchors before blaming the judge |
| Judge–label quadratic-weighted κ | ≥ 0.6 **and** ≥ 0.8 × the ceiling |
| Pass/revise agreement (whole artifact) | ≥ 85 %, and ≥ 95 % on hard-fail criteria |
| Judge self-consistency (3 runs, same input) | score std ≤ 0.5 |

## Bias checks

| Bias | Check | Fail condition |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Correlate score with artifact length within same-label groups | \|r\| > 0.3 |
| Order | Shuffle the order of the upstream documents in the prompt | mean \|Δs\| > 0.3 |
| Self-preference | Judge a sample generated by the judge's own model family against one from another family, with equal labels | mean Δs > 0.3 |
| Verbosity of rationale | Score is higher when the artifact has more `## Notes` prose | \|r\| > 0.3 |
| Anchoring on lint | Lint-clean but bad fixtures (`judge` suite) get high scores | any hard-fail fixture passes |

## Lifecycle

- **Recalibrate** whenever a rubric, judge prompt or judge model changes. The version is in `pipeline.lock.yaml → eval_judge`.
- **Watch for drift:** every month, humans re-label 10 live gate decisions per artifact kind. If pass/revise agreement falls below target, the affected criteria go back to advisory until recalibrated.
- `rubrics/*.yaml` records each criterion's status (`advisory | calibrated`) and its last κ. Only calibrated criteria count towards the gate's weighted score (scoring.md §D).
